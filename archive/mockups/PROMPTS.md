# Maquettes de site (images de proposition) — Tech Point Paris

Prompts prêts à coller dans un modèle d'image récent (GPT image, Gemini 2.5 Flash Image,
Imagen 3/4…) pour générer des **maquettes de site entières**, comme propositions visuelles.
Les modèles récents gèrent bien le texte court : les vrais libellés (nom, accroche, boutons)
sont donc écrits dans les prompts.

## Le site à représenter (résumé)

- **Business** : Tech Point Paris, réparation de smartphones et tablettes + vente, Paris 12,
  quartier Montgallet.
- **Style visé** : e-commerce moderne façon LDLC. Header bleu marine avec recherche, méga-nav,
  hero promo à accent orange, tuiles catégories avec photos produits, tuiles thématiques
  colorées, badges garantie, cartes blanches arrondies. Propre, rassurant, énergique.
- **Palette** : marine `#1b2a4a`, cyan `#18b6d8`, orange `#ff6a1a`, fond gris clair, cartes blanches.

## Proposition A — "LDLC-like" (recommandée)

```
UI/UX web design mockup, full desktop landing page screenshot of a modern French e-commerce
style website for a smartphone and tablet repair shop. All on-screen text must be spelled
exactly as written here, in French.
Top thin orange promotional bar reading: "Écran cassé ? Diagnostic gratuit en boutique, Paris 12".
Dark navy-blue header: on the left a small cyan rounded square logo tile with the letters "TP"
and next to it the wordmark "Tech Point Paris"; center a wide white rounded search bar with a
dropdown labelled "Tous les univers" and placeholder text "Rechercher un service, une marque,
un modèle"; right side three small line icons labelled "Boutique", "Compte", "Appeler".
A secondary navy mega-navigation bar with tabs: "Réparation" (highlighted in cyan), "Écran",
"Batterie", "Vente", "Accessoires", "Nous trouver", each with a thin line icon.
Hero is a large white rounded card split in two: left half has a small orange pill reading
"Réparation express", a bold dark headline "Écran cassé ? On répare votre téléphone" with the
words "On répare" highlighted in orange, a short grey subtitle "Smartphones et tablettes au
cœur de Montgallet, métro Reuilly-Diderot", and a large orange rounded button "Appeler le
01 75 51 02 15"; right half is a clean bright photograph of a technician's hands repairing a
smartphone with a cracked screen on a tidy workshop bench, soft studio lighting.
Below, a row of four white rounded category tiles, each with a device photo, a small navy
circular arrow button, and titles: "Réparation d'écran", "Batterie & charge", "Vente de
téléphones", "Accessoires". Then three colourful rounded feature tiles (blue "Seconde vie",
purple "Garantie", red "Nous trouver").
Clean, airy, professional, trustworthy, high-end tech retail feel. Navy blue, cyan, orange
accents, light grey background, white cards, flat modern UI, crisp realistic web design,
high detail, 4k. No competitor brand logos, no watermark.
```
Ratio conseillé : **3:2** (hero net) ou **9:16** (page longue).

## Proposition B — "Premium clair" (haut de gamme, épuré)

```
UI/UX web design mockup, desktop landing page screenshot for a premium smartphone and tablet
repair service. All text in French, spelled exactly as written.
Warm off-white background, generous whitespace, editorial feel. Minimal header: wordmark
"Tech Point Paris" on the left, a single dark rounded button "01 75 51 02 15" on the right.
Large hero: a small uppercase label "Réparation smartphone & tablette, Paris 12", a big elegant
serif headline "Votre téléphone, réparé avec soin" with the word "réparé" in italic deep-green,
a short grey subline "Diagnostic sur place, devis clair, travail garanti", and two buttons
"Appeler la boutique" and "Venir nous voir".
An asymmetric bento grid of white cards with ultra-soft shadows: one large card with a clean
photo of a repaired smartphone, one card with five gold stars and a short customer quote, small
cards with a shield "Garantie" icon and an address "73 rue de Reuilly". Muted pastel tag chips.
Refined, calm, high-end, Apple-like restraint. Warm off-white, charcoal text, one deep-green
accent, soft pastel chips. Crisp realistic web design, high detail, 4k. No competitor brand
logos, no watermark.
```
Ratio conseillé : **3:2**.

## Comment générer

Colle le prompt dans ton outil (GPT image, Gemini, Imagen…), ratio 3:2. Ces maquettes servent à
choisir une direction ; la version exacte et interactive reste le site codé
(`concepts/concept-7-ldlc.html`).
